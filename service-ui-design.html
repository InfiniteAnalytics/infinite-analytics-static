UI Design & Prototyping

Pixel-perfect visual design, scalable design systems and interactive Figma prototypes — turning research into something people actually want to use.

Our approach

Design that does more than look good

Beautiful design is table stakes. What separates great design from mediocre design is whether it makes the product easier, more intuitive and more enjoyable to use. We design interfaces that achieve all three.

Our design process is iterative and collaborative. We don’t disappear for three weeks and return with a finished product — we show work early, gather feedback often and refine until the design is exactly right.

Visual design

We craft the complete visual language of your product — colour palette, typography, spacing, iconography, illustration and motion. Every visual decision is purposeful: it serves usability, reinforces your brand and creates a coherent, premium feel.

We design for accessibility from the start, not as an afterthought — meeting WCAG guidelines so your product works for everyone.

Design systems

A design system is the foundation that lets your product scale without accumulating debt. We build comprehensive component libraries in Figma — every button, card, form, modal and navigation pattern documented and reusable. This means faster future design iterations and tighter design-to-development handoffs.

Interactive prototyping

Before a single line of code is written, we build high-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma. You can click through the entire product experience, test it with real users and validate that the flow works — all without engineering cost. Stakeholders can see and feel the product before it’s built, which dramatically reduces change requests during development.

Design handoff

We provide engineering teams with meticulously organised Figma files, complete with specs, assets and documentation. We stay available throughout development to answer questions and review implementation — ensuring the final product matches the design intent.

Case Study — iOS & Android

Olobam Fashion & Fabric

A marketplace connecting fashion businesses and fabric suppliers across Australia — designed, built and launched by Infinite Analytics.

App Store Google Play
Industry Fashion & Retail
Platform iOS & Android
Technology React Native
Status Live — 2025
The challenge

A fragmented industry needed a marketplace.

The fashion and fabric industry in Australia is highly fragmented. Fabric suppliers had no reliable digital channel to reach fashion businesses, and designers had no single place to discover suppliers, browse collections and manage relationships.

Olobam set out to change that — creating a two-sided marketplace that brings the whole ecosystem together on a single platform, on mobile.

Our role

End-to-end product ownership.

Infinite Analytics owned every stage of this product — from the initial concept and market research through to UI design, engineering, App Store submission and post-launch support.

This wasn’t a client brief. We built Olobam as our own product, which means we made every decision and carried every responsibility a real product team does.

What we built

A full-featured marketplace platform.

Two user types, one cohesive experience — built for real people in the fashion industry.

Business Storefronts

Suppliers and fashion businesses get their own branded storefront to showcase products, post designs and manage their catalogue.

Discovery & Browse

Personal accounts can browse stores, discover featured designs, filter by category and find exactly what they’re looking for.

In-app Messaging

Buyers and sellers communicate directly through threaded in-app messaging — no need to exchange contact details externally.

Design Posting

Business users can upload and share design images and videos — creating a rich visual catalogue for buyers to explore.

Push Notifications

Real-time alerts for new messages, store activity and platform updates keep users engaged and informed.

Dual Account Types

Separate business and personal account flows, each with tailored interfaces, permissions and feature sets.

Services delivered

Every discipline under one roof.

This project demonstrates what’s possible when research, design and engineering work as a single team from day one — no hand-off friction, no translation loss.
